**The game player bundled with [Bonxie](https://github.com/retr0gr4d3/Bonxie).** Bonxie downloads this release by itself; you don't need to install it separately.

## Skua build 1.6.0-skua.4
- Based on Mugetsu 1.6.0: less memory, no freeze when entering a room, and a macOS version.
- The Skua bridge now works in the macOS build too.
- x86-64-v2 builds for Windows and Linux, for processors without AVX2.
- The player exits cleanly when Bonxie disconnects or its window goes away. Thanks to JonasAlv.
- The player now reports its version as `1.6.0-skua.4`; earlier 1.6.0 Skua builds all said `1.6.0-skua.1`.

Mugetsu 1.6.0 plus `source/patches/skua.patch`:
- `--skua-bridge host:port`: connects to Bonxie, which can then call skua.swf's ExternalInterface callbacks and receives its events. The player exits when Bonxie closes the connection.
- `--skua-parent <window handle>`: draws inside Bonxie's window (a Win32 or X11 window) instead of its own.
- No update check when started by Bonxie (Bonxie pins the player version).

Without those options it behaves like Mugetsu 1.6.0.
